Delete your Farm Orders account

This page is for the "Farm Orders" Android app (com.farmorders.app).

In the app

  1. Open Farm Orders and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

If you still have an order in progress, collect or cancel it first, then delete the account.

Without the app

Email saimithran0911@gmail.com from any address, tell us the mobile number of the account, and we will delete it.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted immediately: your name, your mobile number, your cart, your notifications and your sign-in.
  • Kept: past orders (items, totals, collection dates) for the farm's records, with no name or number attached. They are not linked to you after deletion.
