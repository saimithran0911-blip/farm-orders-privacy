Farm Orders - Privacy Policy

Applies to the Android app "Farm Orders" (com.farmorders.app). Last updated: 26 October 2026.

Farm Orders lets you order fresh produce from the farm and collect it at a time you choose. You pay at the farm when you collect; the app takes no payments. This page explains what the app stores about you and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Mobile number - used as your account sign-in and to identify your orders.
  • Password - stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider; we cannot read it.
  • Name - optional; shown to the farm on your orders.
  • Orders and cart - the items, quantities, collection date and time, notes you type, and the order status.
  • Notifications - messages about your orders (accepted, ready to collect, and so on).

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or advertising identifiers. The app has no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

How it is used

Only to run the service: sign you in, take and prepare your orders, tell you when they are ready, and let the farm contact you about an order. We do not sell or share your information for marketing.

Who can see it

The farm owner and staff can see your name, mobile number and orders so they can prepare and hand over what you ordered. Other customers cannot see your data. Data is stored with our infrastructure provider, Supabase, which hosts the database and sign-in service on our behalf. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time inside the app: Profile, then "Delete my account". This removes your name, mobile number, cart and notifications and signs you out for good. An account with an order still in progress cannot be deleted until that order is collected or cancelled.

Past orders are kept for the farm's own records (for example, to know what was sold), but after deletion they no longer carry your name or number.

If you cannot use the app, email us from the address below and we will delete the account for you.

Children

The app is meant for adults ordering produce and is not directed at children under 13.
